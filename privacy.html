Whatsbnb

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 4 August 2026

Whatsbnb is an AI messaging assistant that helps Airbnb hosts answer guest questions automatically, around the clock. Because our product reads and responds to messages between hosts and their guests, we handle personal data with care. This policy explains what we collect, why, where it lives, how we protect it, and how you can get it deleted.

1. Who we are

Whatsbnb ("we", "us", "our") provides an automated guest-messaging service to Airbnb hosts. The service is operated by Whatsbnb SIA, a company registered in Latvia, at Tērbatas street 32, LV-1011, Riga, Latvia. For any privacy question, contact us at alic@coliving-residences.com. If you are a guest staying at a property managed with Whatsbnb, please note that your host is the party who decides to use our service; we act as a processor on their behalf for guest conversations.

2. What data we collect

We only collect what we need to answer guest questions accurately. This includes:

From hosts (our customers)

  • Account details: name, email address, and login credentials.
  • Listing information you provide so we can answer questions: property address, check-in/check-out instructions, house rules, amenities, Wi-Fi details, local recommendations, and any custom knowledge base or frequently-asked-question content you upload.
  • Billing information needed to process your subscription (handled by our payment processor — see Section 4).

From guests (via the host's Airbnb account)

Whatsbnb connects to your reservations either through a co-host invitation (cohost@whatsbnb.com) or by email forwarding of Airbnb notifications. Through that connection we receive:

  • Guest messages sent through Airbnb and the replies generated or sent on the host's behalf.
  • Reservation details such as guest first name, stay dates, listing name, number of guests, and reservation confirmation codes.

We do not collect guest payment card numbers, government IDs, or full contact details beyond what Airbnb shares in these messages, and we do not ask hosts to provide them.

3. How we use data

We use the data above to: generate and send accurate answers to guest questions; maintain conversation history so replies stay in context; operate, secure, and improve the service; provide customer support; and handle billing. We do not sell personal data, and we do not use guest messages to train third-party public AI models.

4. Where data is stored and who processes it

Data is stored on Google Cloud Platform / Firebase, encrypted in transit (TLS) and at rest. To deliver the service we share the minimum necessary data with a small number of subprocessors:

  • Anthropic (Claude) — guest messages and the relevant listing context are sent to Anthropic's API to generate replies. Anthropic processes this data to return a response and does not use API data to train its models.
  • Google Cloud / Firebase — hosting, database, and authentication.
  • Stripe — subscription billing. We do not store full card numbers on our systems.

Because we serve hosts in both the EU/EEA and the United States, data may be transferred and processed in the United States. Where required, such transfers rely on Standard Contractual Clauses or equivalent safeguards.

5. Upcoming: WhatsApp Business support

We are building a version of Whatsbnb that answers guest questions over WhatsApp Business, using Meta's WhatsApp Business Platform. This feature is not yet live; when it launches, this policy will describe it in the present tense and Meta will be listed as a subprocessor in Section 4. To be accurate about what encryption does and does not cover: messages sent over WhatsApp are encrypted in transit by Meta's platform, but once a message reaches Whatsbnb so we can read it and generate a reply, it is decrypted and handled under this policy — including being sent to Anthropic to produce a response and stored on Google Cloud / Firebase. In other words, WhatsApp/Meta encryption protects messages while they travel to us; it does not by itself protect the data once we process or store it. The protections in Section 6 apply to that data.

6. How we protect data

We apply encryption in transit and at rest, role-based access controls that limit who on our team can view data, authenticated access to all systems, and regular review of the third parties we rely on. Access to guest conversations is restricted to what is needed to operate and support the service. No system is perfectly secure, but we work to keep the amount of data we hold small and the safeguards around it strong.

7. How long we keep data

We retain host account and listing data for as long as your account is active. We retain guest conversation and reservation data only as long as needed to provide the service and for a limited period afterward for support and legal purposes, after which it is deleted or anonymized. When a host closes their account, we delete or anonymize associated data within 30 days, except where we are legally required to keep certain records (for example, billing records).

8. Your rights and how to request deletion

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, export, or delete your personal data, to object to or restrict certain processing, and to withdraw consent. EU/EEA residents have these rights under the GDPR; California residents have comparable rights under the CCPA/CPRA, including the right not to be discriminated against for exercising them.

To request deletion or exercise any right, email alic@coliving-residences.com from the address associated with your account, or, if you are a guest, contact your host or email us and we will coordinate with them. We respond within 30 days. Guests can also stop data collection at any time by asking their host to remove Whatsbnb as a co-host or stop forwarding, which ends our access to that conversation.

9. Children

Whatsbnb is not directed to children under 16 and we do not knowingly collect their data.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as the product evolves. We will post the revised version here and update the "Last updated" date. Material changes affecting hosts will be communicated by email.

11. Contact

Questions or complaints: alic@coliving-residences.com. EU/EEA residents also have the right to lodge a complaint with their local data protection authority.
